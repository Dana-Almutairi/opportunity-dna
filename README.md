## 🌍 The Problem

Every year, students, graduates, and aspiring developers encounter a growing number of hackathons, bootcamps, and technology programs.

But discovering an opportunity is only the beginning.

Many applicants struggle with three questions:

- **Where do I start?** Finding opportunities relevant to their interests and skills can be overwhelming.
- **Am I ready?** Understanding how existing skills align with an opportunity is not always straightforward.
- **What should I learn next?** Turning a skills gap into an actionable preparation plan can be difficult.

The result is a disconnect between **discovering opportunities** and **becoming prepared for them**.

## 💡 The Solution

**Opportunity DNA** brings opportunity discovery, skills assessment, interactive simulation, and learning guidance into one experience.

Instead of simply browsing opportunities, users can explore how their current skills align with different programs and see how developing new skills could improve that alignment.

The core idea is simple:

> Don't just find your next opportunity. Discover what it takes to become ready for it.

| 🏆 **Progress Leaderboard** | Display a progress-oriented leaderboard | Encourage engagement |

### The Feature That Defines Opportunity DNA

**What-If Lab** is a central part of the experience.

Imagine a student who wants to join a technology hackathon but is unsure which skills to develop.

With Opportunity DNA, the student can:

1. Select an opportunity.
2. Review the current skill-match score.
3. Simulate learning an additional skill.
4. Observe how the compatibility score changes.
5. Explore a preparation path informed by the identified skills.

This transforms a static match result into an interactive learning decision.

**Important:** Compatibility scores are illustrative skill-alignment indicators, not predictions of admission or acceptance.

## 🧠 Product Thinking & Design Decisions

Opportunity DNA was designed around a practical question:

**How can we help someone move from uncertainty about an opportunity to a clearer preparation plan?**

The product experience follows five connected stages:

```text
DISCOVER
   |
   v
MY DNA
   |
   v
SMART MATCH
   |
   v
WHAT-IF LAB
   |
   v
MY PATH
```

### Design Principles

**1. Actionable discovery**

Opportunity discovery should lead to useful next steps, rather than ending with a list of programs.

**2. Skills-first exploration**

A personal skills profile creates a starting point for exploring relevant opportunities.

**3. Interactive experimentation**

The What-If Lab allows users to explore hypothetical skill improvements before deciding what to study.

**4. Explainable guidance**

The experience connects opportunity compatibility with skill development, making the next step easier to understand.

**5. Accessible experience**

Bilingual support helps make the prototype more approachable to Arabic- and English-speaking users.
